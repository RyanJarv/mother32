## How To: Play the Keyboard & Create a Sequence

![How to play the keyboard and create a sequence](assets/patchbook/patchbook-03.png)

### Play the Keyboard

1. Hold `(SHIFT)` and press the `(KB)` button to ensure you are in Keyboard mode.
2. Use the OCTAVE/LOCATION buttons to select a higher or lower octave.
3. Play the keyboard.

### Create a Sequence

1. Clear Sequence.
2. Record Sequence.
3. Enter Notes.
4. Finish Recording.
5. Begin Playback.
